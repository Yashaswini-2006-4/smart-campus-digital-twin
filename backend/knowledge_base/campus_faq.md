\# Smart Campus Digital Twin - Campus Knowledge Base



\## Campus Overview

The Smart Campus Digital Twin is a system for monitoring and

analyzing campus conditions using data and AI/ML.



\## Digital Twin

The Digital Twin provides information about campus occupancy,

energy usage, environmental conditions, and maintenance.



\## AI Features

The Smart Campus Digital Twin includes:

\- Occupancy prediction

\- Energy prediction

\- Anomaly detection

\- AI recommendations

\- What-if simulation

\- Campus Health Score



\## Occupancy Prediction

Occupancy prediction estimates campus or classroom occupancy

using the available data and prediction model.



\## Energy Prediction

Energy prediction estimates energy usage using the available

data and prediction model.



\## Anomaly Detection

Anomaly detection identifies unusual patterns in the available

campus data.



\## AI Recommendations

AI recommendations provide suggestions based on the available

campus data and analysis.



\## What-If Simulation

What-if simulation allows users to explore how changes to

selected inputs may affect predicted outcomes.



\## Campus Health Score

The Campus Health Score summarizes selected campus indicators

into a score.
